Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

VerveIn ("the app," "we," "us") is an adaptive fitness app. This page explains, plainly, what data the app actually collects, what stays on your device, and why.

What we collect

The only personal data VerveIn collects and transmits off your device is:

  • Email address — used to create your account and sign you in via a one-time code (email OTP). We use Supabase to handle authentication; your email is stored with them for this purpose.
  • Account ID — an identifier Supabase assigns to your account, used to keep you signed in.

That's the complete list. We don't collect your name, phone number, physical address, or any other contact information.

What stays on your device

Everything else VerveIn works with — your profile, workout and session history, weight log, energy check-ins, health conditions, movement restrictions, and the app's learned calibration — is stored locally on your device only. None of it is transmitted to us or to any third party. If you delete the app or use the in-app data deletion tools, this data is gone.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, VerveIn reads your recent activity, sleep, and resting heart rate to give more honest context alongside how you say you feel. This data is used entirely on your device to adjust that day's plan — it is never transmitted anywhere, never stored on our servers, and never used for advertising. VerveIn never writes anything back to Health.

What we don't do

  • We don't use advertising or analytics SDKs of any kind.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share your data with data brokers or advertisers.
  • We don't use your email or account ID for anything beyond signing you in.

Your data, your control

Inside the app, under Settings, you can:

  • Export your data — get a full copy of everything stored locally, as a file you can keep.
  • Delete your local data — wipe everything stored on your device and start fresh.
  • Delete your account — permanently remove your Supabase account (email, account ID) in addition to your local data.

Children's privacy

VerveIn is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
