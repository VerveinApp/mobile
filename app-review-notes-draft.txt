SIGN-IN FOR REVIEW
VerveIn uses passwordless sign-in only (no username/password accounts exist).
The fastest way in: tap "Continue with Apple" on the sign-in screen — this
works immediately with your own Apple ID, no demo credentials needed.
"Continue with Google" is also available as an alternative.
If you sign in with email instead, a 6-digit code is emailed to that address
(valid 15 minutes) — this requires access to that inbox, so Apple or Google
sign-in is the recommended path for review.

After first sign-in, the app leads through a short onboarding questionnaire
(training goal, experience level, available equipment, weekly schedule)
before reaching the main app.

APP DESCRIPTION / TARGET AUDIENCE
VerveIn is an adaptive fitness training app. It builds a personalized
workout plan from the user's stated goals and equipment, then adjusts that
plan session-to-session based on self-reported readiness (energy, soreness,
sleep) and, optionally, real Apple Health data (steps, resting heart rate,
sleep, active energy). Target audience: general fitness users, from
beginners to experienced lifters, who want a training plan that responds to
how they're actually doing rather than a fixed static program.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES USED
- Supabase — account authentication and cross-device profile sync
- RevenueCat — subscription/entitlement management for VerveIn Plus
- Sign in with Apple / Google — social authentication
- Apple HealthKit — optional, user-initiated; reads steps, resting heart
  rate, sleep, and active energy to inform training readiness; can write
  completed workouts back to Health. Off by default, disconnectable anytime
  in Settings.
- Expo push notification infrastructure — delivers locally-scheduled
  workout reminders
