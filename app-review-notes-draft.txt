SIGN-IN FOR REVIEW
VerveIn uses passwordless sign-in only (no username/password accounts exist), so no demo credentials are needed. Tap "Continue with Apple" on the sign-in screen — it works immediately with your own Apple ID. "Continue with Google" also works. Email sign-in sends a 6-digit code to that inbox (valid 15 minutes), so Apple or Google is the recommended path. After first sign-in, a short onboarding questionnaire (goal, experience, equipment, weekly schedule) leads into the main app.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Settings > Privacy & Account > Delete Account permanently deletes the account and everything stored for it on our servers.

APP DESCRIPTION / TARGET AUDIENCE
VerveIn is an adaptive fitness training app. It builds a workout plan from the user's goals and equipment, then adjusts each session to self-reported readiness (energy, soreness, sleep) and, optionally, Apple Health data (steps, resting heart rate, sleep, active energy). It's for general fitness users, beginners to experienced lifters, who want a plan that responds to how they're actually doing instead of a fixed program.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES USED
- Supabase: account authentication and cross-device profile sync
- RevenueCat: subscription management for VerveIn Plus
- Sign in with Apple / Google: authentication
- Apple HealthKit: optional, user-initiated; reads steps, resting heart rate, sleep and active energy, and can write completed workouts. Off by default; disconnect anytime in Settings.
- Expo push notifications: a notice when someone joins with your referral code, and an occasional re-engagement reminder (at most weekly). Workout reminders are scheduled on the device. Notification permission is only asked in context (the Settings reminder toggle, or a one-time offer after the first completed session), never on launch.
- Sentry: crash and error reporting.

SUBSCRIPTIONS
VerveIn Plus (monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions). Open the paywall from Settings > VerveIn Plus > Get VerveIn Plus, or tap any "... is part of VerveIn Plus" card; it also appears once after the third check-in. It shows each plan's price and period, that it renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the period ends, how to cancel, links to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, and Restore Purchases. The 3-day free trial is on the monthly plan only, and its label only appears when the Apple ID is eligible.
Referrals: an account less than a week old can enter a friend's code once (Settings > VerveIn Plus > Bring a Training Partner); both accounts get 7 days of VerveIn Plus as a RevenueCat promotional entitlement that expires on its own and never charges. Codes can't be bought and have no cash value; all paid access is the In-App Purchase subscription above.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
VerveIn is currently offered only on the United States App Store. Features and content are the same for every user.
