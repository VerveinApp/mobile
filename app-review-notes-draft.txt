SIGN-IN FOR REVIEW
VerveIn uses passwordless sign-in only (no username/password accounts exist).
The fastest way in: tap "Continue with Apple" on the sign-in screen — this
works immediately with your own Apple ID, no demo credentials needed.
"Continue with Google" is also available as an alternative.
If you sign in with email instead, a 6-digit code is emailed to that address
(valid 15 minutes) — this requires access to that inbox, so Apple or Google
sign-in is the recommended path for review.

After first sign-in, the app leads through a short onboarding questionnaire
(training goal, experience level, available equipment, weekly schedule)
before reaching the main app.

APP DESCRIPTION / TARGET AUDIENCE
VerveIn is an adaptive fitness training app. It builds a personalized
workout plan from the user's stated goals and equipment, then adjusts that
plan session-to-session based on self-reported readiness (energy, soreness,
sleep) and, optionally, real Apple Health data (steps, resting heart rate,
sleep, active energy). Target audience: general fitness users, from
beginners to experienced lifters, who want a training plan that responds to
how they're actually doing rather than a fixed static program.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES USED
- Supabase — account authentication and cross-device profile sync
- RevenueCat — subscription/entitlement management for VerveIn Plus
- Sign in with Apple / Google — social authentication
- Apple HealthKit — optional, user-initiated; reads steps, resting heart
  rate, sleep, and active energy to inform training readiness; can write
  completed workouts back to Health. Off by default, disconnectable anytime
  in Settings.
- Expo push notification service — delivers two kinds of remote push
  (a notice when someone joins with your referral code, and an occasional
  re-engagement nudge after several days away, at most weekly); workout
  reminders themselves are scheduled locally on the device. Notification
  permission is only requested in context (the Settings reminder toggle,
  or a one-time offer after the user's first completed session), never on
  launch.
- Sentry — crash and error reporting.

SUBSCRIPTIONS
VerveIn Plus (monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions) is offered on
the paywall. To open it: Settings > VERVEIN PLUS > Get VerveIn Plus, or tap
any "… is part of VerveIn Plus" lock card in the app; it also appears once,
automatically, after the third check-in. The paywall states the price and
period of the selected plan, that it renews automatically unless canceled at
least 24 hours before the period ends, how to cancel, and links to the Terms
of Service and Privacy Policy; Restore Purchases is on the same screen. The
free trial (3 days) is on the monthly plan only; the yearly plan has none. A
free-trial label only appears when the Apple ID is actually eligible for it.

Referral rewards: an account less than a week old can enter a friend's
referral code once (Settings > VERVEIN PLUS > Bring a Training Partner). The
new account and the code's owner each get 7 days of VerveIn Plus, granted
server-side as a RevenueCat promotional entitlement that expires on its own
and never charges; the code's owner gets one such grant per friend who
redeems. Codes can't be bought and have no cash value. No paid access is
sold outside In-App Purchase — every paid path is the StoreKit subscription
above.
